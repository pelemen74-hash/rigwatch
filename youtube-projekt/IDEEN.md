# 30 Story-Ideen: "In this world ..." + Twist

Jede Zeile ist ein fertiger Einstieg für den Master-Prompt.
Format: Regel → Twist.

1. In this world, you can't lie → the hero was the only one who could, and used it to save everyone
2. In this world, everyone has a timer over their head → the timer counts the days until they meet their best friend, not death
3. In this world, you age backwards → the baby at the end is the grandpa from scene one
4. In this world, nobody can see colors → the hero sees them because they're colorblind the "other way"
5. In this world, you lose a memory every time you lie → the hero forgot their own name to protect a friend
6. In this world, it rains only when someone cries → the whole town cries so the crops survive
7. In this world, you can hear what people think → the "mean" kid thinks only kind things
8. In this world, everyone gets one wish at 18 → the hero wishes for someone else's wish to come true
9. In this world, your shadow shows your true self → the villain's shadow is a scared child
10. In this world, you can't say "no" → the hero learns to say "yes, but later"
11. In this world, gravity switches every hour → the lost cat was on the ceiling the whole time
12. In this world, you swap bodies with whoever you touch → the bully has been the hero since scene three
13. In this world, you can only speak 100 words a day → the hero saves all 100 for one apology
14. In this world, money is time → the "rich" kid gives away years to buy a friend one more day
15. In this world, every door leads somewhere random → the last door leads home
16. In this world, you glow when you're happy → the "dark" house is where the hero's mom lives alone
17. In this world, dreams come true at midnight → the hero dreams of a boring day so no one gets hurt
18. In this world, you can pause time once → the hero pauses it to let someone else win
19. In this world, pets can talk for one day a year → the dog says only "stay"
20. In this world, everyone has a superpower except you → the hero's power is making others' powers work
21. In this world, you can't remember faces → the hero recognizes mom by her laugh
22. In this world, the sun never sets → the first night is a gift the hero built
23. In this world, you can trade places with your future self → future self traded first
24. In this world, every lie becomes a scar → the hero is covered in scars for other people's secrets
25. In this world, you can see how many people love you → the hero's number is 1, and it's enough
26. In this world, music is forbidden → the hero's heartbeat is a song
27. In this world, you must say goodbye to one thing a day → today the hero says goodbye to fear
28. In this world, your house moves when you're sad → the hero's house ends up next to a friend's
29. In this world, everyone is born with a key → the hero's key opens other people's doors, not their own
30. In this world, you can rewind one minute a day → the hero never uses it, and tells you why

## Eigene Ideen bauen

Formel: Alltagsding + eine absurde Regel + Figur, die unter der Regel leidet
+ Twist, der die Regel in etwas Gutes verwandelt.
